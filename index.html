01 WHO WE ARE

We believe the best things happen when people come together.

MOVA is an independent event and experience brand based in Podgorica, Montenegro, with a focus on connecting people, businesses and communities across the country.

From professional gatherings and technology events to sports, creative challenges and social experiences, we create opportunities for people to meet, compete, collaborate and discover something new.
